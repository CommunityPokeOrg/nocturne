The files in this directory are taken from flashthing
(https://github.com/JoeyEamigh/flashthing), used under the MIT License:

- superbird.bl2.encrypted.bin — encrypted BL2 stage used to boot a button-held
  "GX-CHIP" (USB mode) Car Thing into USB burn mode.
- superbird.bootloader.img — u-boot blob streamed to the SoC over the AMLC
  handshake during the BL2 sequence.
- stock-meta.json — stock partition restore plan used when the selected
  firmware package has no meta.json (a raw stock dump).
